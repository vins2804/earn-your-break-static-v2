Earn Your Break

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Data stored on your device

Earn Your Break stores focus goals, work and break preferences, focus sessions, break sessions, and daily history locally on your iPhone. This information is used to provide the app's timers, progress, and history features.

Data we collect

Earn Your Break does not require an account and does not collect or upload personal information. We do not use advertising, analytics, or cloud synchronization in the current version of the app.

Data sharing

Because the app's focus data remains on your device, we do not sell, share, or provide that data to third parties.
